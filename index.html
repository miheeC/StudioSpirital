O meni

Moje ime je Elina in verjamem, da imamo v sebi veliko več odgovorov, kot si jih pogosto dovolimo slišati.

Moja pot me je pripeljala do raziskovanja telesa, energije, meditacije, intuicije in različnih načinov notranjega dela.

Danes skozi Spirital ustvarjam izkušnje, ki človeka povabijo nazaj k sebi.

Ne učim te, kdo moraš postati.
Ustvarjam prostor, v katerem lahko lažje začutiš, kdo že si.

Spoznaj mojo zgodbo

My name is Elina, and I believe we carry far more answers within us than we usually allow ourselves to hear.

My path has led me to explore the body, energy, meditation, intuition and many forms of inner work.

Today, through Spirital, I create experiences that invite people back to themselves.

I don't teach you who you must become.
I create a space where you can more easily feel who you already are.
