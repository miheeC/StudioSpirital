Elivital®

Kolikokrat čez dan sploh zares začutiš svoje telo in to, kaj ti sporoča?

Večino časa smo ves dan v glavi. Razmišljamo, načrtujemo, rešujemo, hitimo.

Telo pa samo čaka, da ga slišimo in prepoznamo njegove signale.

Ali pa si v drugi skrajnosti, ko opaziš vsako malenkost, vsako nepravilnost na svojem telesu in imaš občutek, da ga moraš »popraviti«, spremeniti …

Elivital® je moja avtorska praksa, ki te skozi gibanje, dih, prisotnost in energijske tehnike povabi nazaj v stik s svojim telesom.

Ne gre za klasično vadbo.
Gre za čas, ki ga nameniš sebi.

How many times a day do you truly feel your body and what it is telling you?

Most of the day we live in our heads. Thinking, planning, solving, rushing.

And the body simply waits for us to hear it and recognise its signals.

Or perhaps you're at the other extreme — noticing every little thing, every imperfection in your body, and feeling that you have to "fix" it, change it …

Elivital® is my own practice which, through movement, breath, presence and energy techniques, invites you back into connection with your body.

It isn't a classic workout.
It's time you give to yourself.

Kaj je Elivital®?

Elivital® povezuje različne pristope gibanja in notranjega dela v celostno prakso. V njej se prepletajo:

Elivital® weaves different approaches to movement and inner work into one holistic practice. It brings together:

  • gibanje
  • elementi joge
  • elementi pilatesa
  • dihalne tehnike
  • raztezanje
  • energijske tehnike
  • sproščanje
  • prisotnost

Vsaka praksa je nekoliko drugačna, skupna pa ji je ena stvar:

da se za trenutek ustaviš, ponovno začutiš sebe in lahkotno gradiš svojo notranjo moč.

Every practice is a little different, but they all share one thing:

you pause for a moment, feel yourself again and gently build your inner strength.

Kaj lahko pričakuješ?

Na Elivital® praksi bomo:

  • razgibali in prebudili telo,
  • začutili moč in stabilnost,
  • povezovali gibanje z dihom,
  • sproščali napetosti,
  • usmerjali pozornost v telo,
  • raziskovali pretok energije,
  • zaključili z umiritvijo in sprostitvijo.

Praksa je prilagojena skupini in tvojemu trenutnemu počutju.

Ni pomembno, koliko znaš.
Ni pomembno, kako gibčna si.
In ni pomembno, kdaj si nazadnje telovadila.

Pridi takšna, kot si.

In an Elivital® practice we will:

  • loosen up and awaken the body,
  • feel strength and stability,
  • connect movement with breath,
  • release tension,
  • bring our attention into the body,
  • explore the flow of energy,
  • finish with calm and relaxation.

The practice is adapted to the group and to how you feel right now.

It doesn't matter how much you know.
It doesn't matter how flexible you are.
And it doesn't matter when you last exercised.

Come exactly as you are.

Elivital® ni tekmovanje

Ni pomembno, kdo naredi več ponovitev.
Ni pomembno, kdo je bolj gibčen.
Ni pomembno, kako je videti tvoja poza.

Tukaj se ne primerjamo. Namesto tega se učimo poslušati svoje telo.

Kako se danes počutim?
Kaj moje telo potrebuje?
Kje je moja meja?
Kje lahko popustim?

In prav v tem je bistvo Elivitala®.

It doesn't matter who does more repetitions.
It doesn't matter who is more flexible.
It doesn't matter what your pose looks like.

We don't compare ourselves here. Instead, we learn to listen to our body.

How do I feel today?
What does my body need?
Where is my limit?
Where can I let go?

And that is the very essence of Elivital®.

Za koga je Elivital®?

Elivital® je namenjen ženskam, ki si želijo:

  • več gibanja v svojem vsakdanu,
  • boljši stik s svojim telesom,
  • sprostitve napetosti,
  • več prisotnosti,
  • trenutka zase,
  • nežnega, a aktivnega načina skrbi zase.

Primeren je tudi za začetnice. Vodim te skozi čisto vsak gib in pravilno izvedbo le-tega.

Če že dolgo nisi telovadila, je to čisto v redu. Če si vajena gibanja, lahko v praksi prav tako najdeš prostor za raziskovanje in poglobitev.

Elivital® is for women who want:

  • more movement in their everyday life,
  • a better connection with their body,
  • to release tension,
  • more presence,
  • a moment for themselves,
  • a gentle yet active way of taking care of themselves.

It is also suitable for beginners. I guide you through every single movement and how to do it correctly.

If you haven't exercised in a long time, that's completely fine. If you're used to moving, you'll also find room in the practice to explore and go deeper.

In na koncu?

Morda boš odšla bolj sproščena.
Morda boš čutila več energije.
Morda bo tvoje telo potrebovalo počitek.
Morda boš prvič po dolgem času opazila, kako se v resnici počutiš.

Ni treba, da nekaj posebnega dosežeš.

Dovolj je, da si vzameš čas zase.

Maybe you'll leave more relaxed.
Maybe you'll feel more energy.
Maybe your body will need rest.
Maybe, for the first time in a long while, you'll notice how you really feel.

You don't have to achieve anything special.

It's enough to take time for yourself.
