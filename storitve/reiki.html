    O reikiju

    Reiki je japonska tradicija sproščanja, pri kateri praktičarka z nežnim dotikom rok spremlja tvoje telo, ti pa v tem času preprosto počivaš.

    Reiki is a Japanese relaxation tradition in which the practitioner gently accompanies your body with her hands, while you simply rest.

    V Studiu Spirital reiki razumemo predvsem kot prostor za upočasnitev — čas, v katerem lahko za trenutek odložiš vsakodnevni tempo in si vzameš prostor zase. Seansa poteka v tišini ali ob nežni glasbi, po želji pa se pred ali po njej tudi pogovoriva o tvoji izkušnji.

    At Studio Spirital, we see reiki primarily as a space for slowing down — time in which you can, for a moment, set aside the pace of everyday life and make room for yourself. The session takes place in silence or with gentle music, and if you'd like, we can talk about your experience before or after it.

    Reiki v Studio Spirital

    Reiki je več kot samo polaganje rok na telo.
    Je ustvarjanje prostora, v katerem lahko za trenutek odložiš vse, kar nosiš s sabo.
    Prostor, kjer ti ni treba biti močna, produktivna ali ves čas nekaj reševati.
    Samo prideš. Se uležeš. Zadihaš. In si dovoliš prejeti.
    Vsaka seansa je drugačna, ker je drugačna tudi vsaka ženska, ki pride skozi vrata Studio Spirital.

    Reiki is more than just laying hands on the body.
    It's creating a space where you can set down, for a moment, everything you carry with you.
    A space where you don't have to be strong, productive, or constantly solving something.
    You simply arrive. Lie down. Breathe. And allow yourself to receive.
    Every session is different, because every woman who walks through the doors of Studio Spirital is different.

    Seansa je namenjena sprostitvi, umiritvi in osebni izkušnji ter ne predstavlja zdravstvene obravnave oziroma ne nadomešča zdravstvene oskrbe.

    This session is intended for relaxation, calming and personal experience, and does not constitute medical treatment or replace medical care.
